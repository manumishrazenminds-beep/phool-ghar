Flower e-commerce and daily subscription platform

A responsive web application for selling flowers online and delivering them every morning: customer shop, daily subscription, admin panel and delivery team view.

How it fits together

One connected system

Each part needs the others. That is why the scope is priced as one build and not as options you can drop.

Database and security

Feeds: Every other part

Nothing can be saved without it, and without access rules one customer could see another’s orders.

Accounts

Feeds: Checkout, plans, notifications

Orders and plans must belong to a person, and reminders need somewhere to go.

Checkout with online payments

Feeds: Orders, invoices, admin

An order is confirmed only after a verified payment. Without that check, fake or duplicate orders get through.

Subscription engine

Feeds: Nightly deliveries, calendar

A paid plan produces no flowers until the nightly engine creates each delivery on time.

Notifications

Feeds: Plans, deliveries, payments

Skipping a day before cut-off only works if the customer is reminded in time.

Admin and stock

Feeds: Storefront, deliveries

Stock decides what can be bought, and the delivery list is how tomorrow’s orders get packed.

Delivery screens

Feeds: Customer order tracking

Riders’ updates are what customers see on the order timeline.

Responsive build and SEO

Feeds: The whole storefront

Customers arrive from phones and search. A site that is hard to use or find earns nothing.
